GG’APP: DATA POINTS:
These data points are well suited to the framework of our GG’APP health intelligence platform. The data points will support our operational monitoring, executive decision-making, actuarial analysis, provider network management, and predictive analytics for extended third parties, including medical schemes, insurers, employers, and government health agencies.

WHY: THE VALUE OF THESE DATA POINTS FOR GG’APP
-	Data Mining for customer behavioural insights 
-	Dashboard Admin reporting
-	Product redesign guidance
-	Marketing Campaign messaging & targeted collateral
-	Customer-informed sales propositions
-	Targeted Customer satisfaction surveys 
-	Media Advertising aligned to customer preferences
FOUR STRATEGIC DATA POINTS
1.	Disease Burden Intelligence: 
•	Nature of treatment frequently sought
•	Total patients treated monthly and annually 
•	Most utilized type of medical services (e.g., medication, laboratory, etc Average Cost Per Provider
•	Preferred providers for specific treatment pathways
•	Disease Dashboard Visuals (Interactive maps showing disease burden by county)
•	Acute Disease Burden
•	Chronic Disease Burden
•	Mental Health Cases
•	Maternal Health Cases
•	Child Health Cases 
•	Provider utilization benchmarks - per country/ region and per type of service rendered
•	Most prevalent times when treatment is sought (day/night)
•	Top Disease Categories eg Malaria, Diabetes, Hypertension
•	Average cost of treatment comparisons by provider
•	Average Cost Per Provider

2.	Membership Demographics
•	Age profiling and analysis of applicants (below <18 years and above >19 years – indicated within five age bands: 
Eg 9-24: 25-30...etc)
•	Age profiling and analysis of beneficiaries (below <18 years and above >19 years) – indicated within five age bands: 
Eg 9-24: 25-30...etc)
•	Registered customers' gender profile (Male/ Female)
•	Disease prevalence by age and above
3.	Financial Highlights
•	Revenue & Expense Dashboards
•	Other Revenue eg advertising of App
•	Predictive Analytics (AI)
•	Financial trends – Annual credit threshold ranges applied for
•	Applicants by Region/ Country
•	Most preferred loan repayment options by customers
•	Medical Inflation Dashboard: Tracking utilization, technology, pharmaceuticals, provider tariffs, and disease complexity drive medical inflation. General inflation in Kenya was about 4–5% through much of 2025, but healthcare cost inflation often exceeds headline inflation.  
4.	Consumer Health
•	Member Satisfaction with App Services
•	Most preferred provider (from provider reviews by customers)
